ॐ Mantra Mala — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Mantra Mala ("the app") is a japa (mantra) counter for Android, published by sjpixels.com. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: the app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

What the app stores

To work, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • Your current bead count and when the current round started.
  • Your completed malas (date, time, duration, which mantra, and whether you chanted it or played it from a voice sample).
  • Mantras you add yourself.
  • Voice samples you record in the app or choose from your files, and the count of how many times each has been played. Recordings are saved in the app's private storage on your phone and are never uploaded or shared.
  • Your preferences: theme, colours, counter style, language, daily goal, vibration, bell, volume-key and screen-on settings, and the floating bead's colour, size and position.

This information never leaves your device through the app. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send data to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

Link to our website

The app shows "Built by sjpixels.com". Tapping it opens sjpixels.com in your web browser. The app itself sends nothing; what happens on the website is covered by that site's own policy.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android's own backup (for example, Google One backup) or you move to a new phone with Android's transfer tool, Android may include the app's data in that backup so your history is restored. Voice recordings are left out of cloud backup and only move with a direct phone-to-phone transfer. This is controlled by you in your phone's settings and is handled by Google/Android under their privacy terms. We never receive or have access to it.

Permissions

  • Vibrate: for the gentle vibration on each bead and at the end of each mala. You can turn it off in the app.
  • Display over other apps: only if you turn on Keep over other apps for the floating counter bead, so the bead can stay on screen while you use other apps. The bead only shows your count; the app cannot see or read anything in other apps.
  • Foreground service and notifications: used only while the floating bead is kept over other apps, so Android lets it keep running. It shows an ongoing notification with your count and a Turn off button.
  • Microphone: asked for only when you tap Record to record a voice sample of a mantra. Audio is recorded only while the recording screen is open and you have started recording, is saved only on your phone, and is never sent anywhere (the app has no internet permission). You can also skip this and choose an audio file instead.
  • Media playback in the background: while you play a voice sample, a foreground service keeps it playing and counting when the screen is off or you use other apps. It shows a notification with the count and Pause/Stop buttons, and ends as soon as you stop playback.

Deleting your data

Your history is kept for as long as the app is installed. You can delete any entry or a whole day, or use Clear past history (everything before today, older than 30 days, older than 1 year, or everything) on the History screen. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes all of its data from your device. Voice samples can be deleted one by one from the voice samples list on the Japa screen.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.
