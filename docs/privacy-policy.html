ॐ Mantra Mala — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Mantra Mala ("the app") is a japa (mantra) counter for Android, published by [YOUR NAME OR STUDIO NAME]. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: the app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

What the app stores

To work, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • Your current bead count and when the current round started.
  • Your completed malas (date, time, duration and which mantra you chanted).
  • Mantras you add yourself.
  • Your preferences: theme, colours, counter style, language, daily goal, vibration, bell, volume-key and screen-on settings, and the floating bead's colour, size and position.

This information never leaves your device through the app. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send data to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

Link to our website

The app shows "Built by sjpixels.com". Tapping it opens sjpixels.com in your web browser. The app itself sends nothing; what happens on the website is covered by that site's own policy.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android's own backup (for example, Google One backup) or you move to a new phone with Android's transfer tool, Android may include the app's data in that backup so your history is restored. This is controlled by you in your phone's settings and is handled by Google/Android under their privacy terms. We never receive or have access to it.

Permissions

  • Vibrate: for the gentle vibration on each bead and at the end of each mala. You can turn it off in the app.
  • Display over other apps: only if you turn on Keep over other apps for the floating counter bead, so the bead can stay on screen while you use other apps. The bead only shows your count; the app cannot see or read anything in other apps.
  • Foreground service and notifications: used only while the floating bead is kept over other apps, so Android lets it keep running. It shows an ongoing notification with your count and a Turn off button.

Deleting your data

Your history is kept for as long as the app is installed. You can delete any entry or a whole day, or use Clear past history (everything before today, older than 30 days, older than 1 year, or everything) on the History screen. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes all of its data from your device.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.
