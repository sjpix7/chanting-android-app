## Listing rules to stay within (common rejection causes)

- No "best", "#1", "top", "free", prices, or calls to action ("download now") in the title, icon, or feature graphic.
- Don't use other apps' names or trademarks, and don't stuff keywords.
- Screenshots must show the real app (ours are real captures in a decorative frame).
